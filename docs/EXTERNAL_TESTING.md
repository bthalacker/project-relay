# Readiness for Future External Testing

**Preparation only. External testing and media intake are not open.**

Project Relay is pre-alpha. A possible conversation with an organization does
not establish a partnership, endorsement, pilot commitment or permission to
receive content. Do not send or attach private media, project files, personal
data or credentials to public GitHub issues.

## Before inviting a participant

The following arrangements must be defined and agreed before accepting outside
video or audio. They describe required future processes, not an operational
data-handling service or an existing privacy guarantee.

| Requirement | What must be settled |
| --- | --- |
| Supported workflow | Assess the editor/version, project structure, media types, intended changes and known limitations. Confirm support before promising a test. |
| Permission and ownership | Obtain explicit permission to use the submitted video/audio for the agreed test; establish who owns it and what usage rights are granted. Address other people's identifiable content where applicable. |
| Scope of use | Agree whether media may be analyzed, edited or retained as an example. Permission for a test does not grant public-demo, redistribution or future-training rights. |
| Private handling | Choose an approved private transfer/storage route, identify who may access originals/projects and explain any proposed processing services before use. Public issues are not an intake route. |
| Retention and deletion | Define what is retained, for what purpose, where and for how long; how deletion is requested and verified; and what happens to derived samples, backups and feedback records. |
| Isolation and originals | Use disposable test copies. Preserve original footage and participant projects; define verified checkpoints and recovery steps. |
| Limitations and consent | Explain unsupported operations, experimental artistic decisions and the human review required. Obtain agreement to the specific test scope. |
| Feedback and failures | Provide a private route for sensitive reports, a non-sensitive feedback template and clear stop/escalation criteria. Public reports must be sanitized. |

No retention duration, transfer service or intake contact is promised here;
these choices must be established before invitations, not improvised after receipt.
No submitted material may be publicly released or redistributed without separate
authorization.

## Technical entry criteria

- A useful supported workflow is repeatable with reliable navigation and human
  handoffs; unsupported structures are detected before mutation.
- Original media and accepted work survive interrupted saves, rejected proposals
  and the supported recovery paths.
- Editing results can be reviewed with sufficient source-labelled picture/audio
  context. Structural checks and human editorial approval remain distinct.
- Installation, usability, error reporting and data-handling instructions are
  understandable to the intended participant.
- Any performance or effort-saving claims are measured rather than assumed.

## A future feedback record

Record the participant's editing problem, intended outcome, supported setup,
observed result, reproduction steps and severity. Request only the minimum
agreed evidence. Do not put original media or sensitive project details in
public tickets. A failed or safely blocked operation must remain visible in
the test results rather than being counted as success.
