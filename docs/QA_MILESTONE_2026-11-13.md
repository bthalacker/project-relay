# November 13, 2026 — Approved Edit Reproduction

**Pre-alpha · No public download or announced beta or release date**

## Milestone overview

Relay reproduced a complex human-approved transition in a disposable editing
project. This extends the [October 27 milestone](QA_MILESTONE_2026-10-27.md),
whose results remain historical. It is focused engineering evidence, not a
completed product or general autonomous editing qualification.

## Verified editing operations

- Two case-specific unwanted picture-frame corrections were demonstrated. One
  preserved intended linked audio and downstream relationships, with independent
  saved-structure and native picture/audio checks. The second received focused
  native picture verification.
- An approved unwanted detached audio-tail removal preserved surviving picture,
  audio, fades and timeline contributions. A focused native output window was
  checked.
- A complex approved transition reproduced outgoing and incoming story
  boundaries, legitimate overlapping audio, separate fades, removal of unwanted
  opening and ending material, title and visual lead-in removal, legitimate audio
  underneath removed picture, and downstream timeline movement.

## Human-approved reference workflow

A human correction supplies an example whose observed result can be checked and
used for further engineering and regression tests. The observed change does not
by itself reveal artistic intent, authorize a similar edit elsewhere, or
automatically train an AI model. Creative decisions remain with the human editor.

A three-level QC priority design distinguishes low-priority evidence-supported
routine edits for explicit review, medium-priority acceptable standard edits
with optional artistic improvements, and high-priority uncertain story, dialogue,
ownership or consequential choices requiring attention. This is development
design work, not a released public interface or bulk approval feature.

## Saved-state and native-output verification

The final disposable reconstruction matched all 127 checked saved timeline
contributions on source and timeline intervals, tracks, streams, linkage and
fade settings. Focused native picture checks matched approved transition and
title-removal examples at reduced resolution. Decoded source evidence, saved
structure, native output and human editorial approval establish different facts.

Aligned audio-window correlations supported similarity; exact decoded PCM
equivalence for the full composition was not established. The earlier unwanted
native frames have case-specific verified corrections, while the general decoder
or sampling behavior remains unresolved. Native picture and audio correctness
are not universally guaranteed.

## Regression and preservation

The focused November checkpoint recorded **117 regression tests passing** and
**14 protected file hashes matching**. Human references and existing QC decisions
were preserved. No full-project batch rerun or production promotion occurred.
These counts have a different scope from the October campaign and must not be
added to its counts or treated as an overall reliability score.

## Current limitations

Automatic identification of complete editorial story boundaries and
generalization to unfamiliar video remain open. Some inter-scene joins are
incomplete; title and recap removal cases remain unresolved. Artistic audio
choices are not generally qualified. GUI and dialog recovery are only partially
reliable. Broader final-output audiovisual qualification and repeatable end-to-end
autonomous operation remain unfinished.

## Next engineering priorities

Improve boundary and title/recap evidence, qualify audio ownership and human
review workflows, broaden native audiovisual verification, strengthen recovery,
and test supported end-to-end workflows on representative material. Additional
editor adapters remain planned and require separate qualification.

## What this milestone does not demonstrate

The reconstruction does not establish independent artistic judgment, automatic
editing of arbitrary unfamiliar projects, sample-exact full-composition audio,
or a complete unattended workflow. The intended experience remains
**Analyze → Propose → Preview → Review → Execute → Verify → Export**, and is
still in development. Relay is not publicly downloadable; external testing is
not open, and no beta or release date is announced.
