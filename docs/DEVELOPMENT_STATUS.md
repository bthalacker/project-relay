# Development status

Project Relay is in active pre-alpha development. It is a development codename, not the final commercial product name. A limited end-to-end deterministic Filmora workflow now works and has completed live qualification; broader algorithm and product coverage remain under development.

## Demonstrated in pre-alpha

- An editor-neutral semantic edit-plan architecture.
- A limited deterministic Filmora subset qualified through planning, guarded execution, and saved-project verification.
- Project-bound video/audio ownership evidence, with acoustic observations kept distinct from editorial audio ownership.
- Frame-accurate, source-bound structural planning where supported by available evidence.
- Reconciliation of saved project state, checks for unexpected changes to unrelated timeline contributions, and verified rollback/recovery for the qualified workflow.
- Explicit handling of expected Open and Save As dialogs as workflow states.
- Reviewed audio transition support with durations derived from actual overlap geometry.
- More than 200 automated regression checks currently passing, alongside continued comparison with human-reviewed reference results.

These results apply only to the qualified subset. They are not claims of production readiness, support for all Filmora editing, or completion of the full product or broader editing algorithm.

## Currently being expanded

- Broader deterministic structural-edit coverage.
- Remaining retained-audio ownership and preservation decisions.
- Transition/audio qualification and end-to-end project coverage.
- Generalization beyond development examples and appropriate reduction of review-gated cases.
- Reliability, safe failure, recovery, and repeatability qualification.

Uncertainty remains visible and can gate execution for review rather than be silently treated as a decision.

## Future product work

- A complete user-facing graphical workflow.
- Wider editor operation coverage and user-facing onboarding.
- DaVinci Resolve and Adobe Premiere Pro adapters, which are planned for future development only.
- Copyright-safe public demonstrations and examples.

Project Relay is not currently presented as publicly downloadable.
